Status: draft / new optional text only / runtime not implemented

## 0. Purpose

These are **short optional party reactions** tied to towns, work, food, rebuilding, awkward encounters, and imperfect knowledge.

They are not support-conversation replacements and should not fire so often that walking through town becomes a cutscene corridor.

Rules used here:

- companions may disagree without one becoming the author's designated correct answer;
- a character can misunderstand an NPC;
- silence is allowed;
- no skit exists only to explain lore;
- some skits end on practical action rather than an emotional thesis;
- if a scene touches an unresolved relationship (Shanny/Xiao, Alan's father, Luna in Galvania), it is marked for runtime hold even though new prose may be drafted now.

# 9. Trigger philosophy

Recommended trigger types after approval:

- once-per-area optional bark after first conversation with selected new NPC;
- revisit-only barks keyed by existing world flags;
- party-member checks, but no scene should require an exact four-person party unless emotionally necessary;
- no automatic bark on every map entry;
- some interactions intentionally produce **no** companion response.

Avoid turning skits into reward dispensers. Most should give no item, EXP, affinity, or hidden numerical benefit. Their reward is characterization and changed understanding of the place.
